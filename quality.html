Consistency & Inspection

Quality Control

Consistent material quality and inspection throughout processing — from raw material to final packaging.

What We Check

Inspection Items

The properties we verify to keep material within specification.

Chemical Composition

Material composition verified against the required grade.

Hardness

Hardness checked to match the required material condition.

Tensile Properties

Tensile behaviour confirmed for mechanical requirements.

Thickness

Thickness measured across the strip to confirm tolerance.

Width

Width verified against the ordered slit width.

Surface

Surface condition inspected for defects and finish.

Flatness / Straightness

Flatness and straightness checked for downstream processing.

Material Certification

Material documents prepared to accompany each shipment.

Final Inspection

A final check before packaging to confirm readiness for shipment.
