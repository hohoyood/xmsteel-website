What We Do

Processing

From steel coil to finished strip and cut-to-length products — the processing steps behind consistent, ready-to-use material.

Our Processes

Processing Capabilities

Each step is aimed at delivering the material condition, dimensions and surface your application requires.

01

Annealing

Heat treatment that softens the steel and improves workability before further processing.

Value to you Achieves the softness and formability your downstream forming and stamping operations require.
02

Rolling

Rolling of steel strip to achieve the required thickness and dimensional accuracy.

Value to you Delivers controlled thickness that matches your dimensional requirements.
03

Heat Treatment

Controlled heating and cooling to reach the required hardness and mechanical properties.

Value to you Sets the hardness and material condition your application requires.
04

Slitting

Cutting wide coils into narrower strips to the customer's specified width.

Value to you Custom widths from a single coil, reducing waste and shortening lead time.
05

Polishing

Surface finishing to improve the smoothness and appearance of the strip surface.

Value to you Improved surface condition for appearance and performance-critical parts.
06

Cut-to-Length

Uncoiling and cutting steel coil into flat sheets of a specified length.

Value to you Ready-to-use sheets cut to your specified length and width.
07

Packaging

Wrapping, banding and protecting finished coils and sheets for safe transport.

Value to you Secure, seaworthy packaging that protects material in transit.
